The hiring path

Built around how transportation hiring actually works.

A driver candidate passes through several hands before a first route. We coordinate the handoffs and follow up at each step, so candidates don't stall between HR, the department and outside vendors.

  1. ApplicationSourced, screened and invited to apply
  2. InterviewScheduled with your transportation team
  3. OfferYour district decides; we keep the candidate engaged
  4. Pre-employmentDOT physical, drug screen, fingerprinting, background check and references, tracked to completion
  5. TrainingYour district's program, with check-ins until it starts
  6. First routeHired and on the road

Your district and its vendors conduct every screening and make every hiring determination. We track whether steps are complete and keep candidates moving.

Common questions

Do you train drivers or provide CDL training?

No. Your district runs its own training. We recruit trainees for it and help keep them moving through the steps to their start date.

Do the drivers work for URC?

No. We recruit candidates for your district to hire. Your district is the employer, and URC doesn't supply temporary drivers or operate transportation services.

Can you help year-round?

Yes. Many transportation vacancies recur through the year, so we keep sourcing and screening on a steady cadence rather than in one-time pushes.

Where do you work?

We're based in Colorado and support school districts across the US.
