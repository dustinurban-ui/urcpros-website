Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Urban Recruiting & Consulting, LLC ("URC," "we," "us") operates urcpros.com. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, company, work email, phone number, role, company size, industry and anything you write in the form.
  • Scheduling. If you book a call, scheduling is handled by Calendly, which collects the details you enter there under its own privacy policy.
  • Usage data. We use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views and see which pages are useful. It does not use cookies to track you across other websites.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, schedule and prepare for calls, provide our services, and improve this website. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and our business, such as our website host, email delivery provider and scheduling tool, and only as needed for them to provide those services, or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our business records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you've given us by emailing connect@urcpros.com.
