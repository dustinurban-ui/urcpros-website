Common questions

Can we start with just recruiting?

Yes. Many clients do, and add HR support later.

Do we need our own HR person?

No. URC can be your HR department, or support the person you have.

Are you a PEO?

No. Your employees stay on your payroll and you remain the employer. We work as your HR team, alongside your payroll, benefits and insurance providers.

Who makes the final decisions?

You do. We give you recommendations and handle the work, and ownership decides.

Where do you work?

We're based in Colorado and work with businesses across the US.
