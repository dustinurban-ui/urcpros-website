Common questions

How is this different from a staffing agency?

Agencies fill one role at a time and charge per hire. We work as your HR department: recruiting, onboarding, compliance and leadership support for one monthly fee.

What happens on the strategy call?

We spend 30 minutes on your roles, sites and hiring process, show you where it's costing you the most, and tell you honestly whether we're the right fit. No pitch deck.

Can we start with just recruiting?

Yes. Most clients start with the area that's hurting most, usually hiring, and add leadership, HR operations or growth planning later.

What does the Route to Hire portal show us?

Where every candidate stands, from offer through application, DOT physical, drug screen, background check, references and employment verification. It tracks whether each step is done, not the results.
