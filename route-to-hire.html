Route to Hire

Keep Candidates Moving From Application to First Day.

How it works

Four steps, one shared view

  1. 1

    We add your candidates

    URC staff enter or upload candidates and send each one a private checklist link or QR code.

  2. 2

    Candidates confirm their steps

    They mark progress on what applies to the role: application, DOT physical, drug screen, fingerprinting, background check, references and any client-specific requirements.

  3. 3

    Reminders go out automatically

    When a candidate stalls, Route to Hire sends a reminder or re-engagement email. URC staff watch the process and update statuses as needed.

  4. 4

    You see the pipeline

    Your team gets a read-only dashboard with live counts by stage, outstanding steps, and older applicants being re-engaged.

What it changes

Less chasing. Clearer hiring.

  • Fewer disconnected spreadsheets and email chains
  • A clear view of where each candidate stands
  • More consistent follow-up
  • Better coordination between URC, HR and department leaders
  • Earlier warning on candidates who need attention
  • Support for reducing preventable candidate dropout

What Route to Hire doesn't do

Route to Hire tracks whether each step is complete. It doesn't show background check or drug screen results, conduct screenings or make hiring decisions. It works alongside your existing hiring systems and processes, and your organization stays responsible for compliance.
