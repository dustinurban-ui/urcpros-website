Common questions

Do you work with roofing and construction companies outside Colorado?

Yes. We're based in Colorado and work with businesses across the US.

Can you help when hiring picks up quickly?

Yes. We can increase sourcing and screening when you need more candidates, and scale back when things settle.

Are you a PEO?

No. Your employees stay on your payroll and you remain the employer. We work as your HR team, alongside your payroll, benefits and insurance providers.

Who makes the final decisions?

You do. We give you recommendations and handle the work, and ownership decides.
