About Urban Recruiting & Consulting

We're a recruiting and HR consulting firm that helps organizations build the people, processes and infrastructure they need to grow. Since 2014, we've partnered with school districts, construction companies and other operationally complex organizations in transportation, logistics, agriculture and the public sector.

More than filling open positions

We strengthen the infrastructure behind a workforce: recruiting and candidate sourcing, HR systems, compensation, onboarding, leadership support, policies and workforce planning. Clients get experienced recruiting and HR capabilities without building a large internal department. Their own leaders keep making the decisions.

What we bring

  • Full-service recruiting and proactive candidate sourcing
  • High-volume and hard-to-fill recruiting
  • Recruiting process development and optimization
  • HR infrastructure and operational support
  • HRIS and ATS implementation and optimization
  • Compensation benchmarking
  • Onboarding and hiring process support
  • Policy and process development
  • Leadership and management support
  • Workforce strategy and consulting
