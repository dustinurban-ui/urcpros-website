Vacancies don't stay in HR. They show up in operations.

Most districts aren't short on effort. They're short on recruiting capacity, and on someone to keep each candidate moving between HR, the department and pre-employment requirements.

  • Routes go uncovered or get doubled up.
  • Drivers, custodians and kitchen staff cover extra shifts, and overtime grows.
  • Department leaders spend their week on hiring instead of running operations.
  • Essential student services feel the strain.

How we help

An extension of your HR and department teams

Keep candidates coming

  • Maintain a steady flow of candidates for recurring vacancies.
  • Source and screen candidates for weekly interviews.

Keep candidates moving

  • Coordinate interviews and candidate handoffs between HR and the department.
  • Follow up with each candidate from application through hire.
  • Support DOT and pre-employment steps such as physicals, drug screens and background checks, within your district's policies.
  • Spot where applicants stall or drop out, and re-engage them.

Strengthen the process

  • Improve recruiting processes and procedures.
  • Bridge communication gaps between HR and transportation or other departments.
  • Give department leaders clear visibility into the hiring pipeline.

Process

How a district partnership works

  1. 1

    Consultation

  4. 4

    Review and improve

    We look at where candidates stall and adjust the process with your team.

How Route to Hire works

Common questions

Do you replace our HR department?

No. We work alongside your HR and department teams. Your district makes every hiring decision and remains the employer.

Do you provide temporary or substitute workers?

No. We recruit candidates for your district to hire into its own positions.

Do you provide CDL training?

No. We recruit trainees for districts that offer paid CDL training, and help keep them moving through the steps to their start date.

How do you handle DOT and pre-employment requirements?

We coordinate and track steps like DOT physicals, drug screens and background checks so candidates don't stall. Your district and its vendors conduct them and make all determinations.

How is pricing structured?

A predictable monthly fee scoped to the work, with no per-hire fees. You'll get a clear quote after your consultation.

Where do you work?

We're based in Colorado and support school districts across the US.
